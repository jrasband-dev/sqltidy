sqltidy.rules¶

sqltidy.rules.load_rules(rule_type=None)[source]¶

Load all rules from tidy/ and rewrite/ directories, plus any plugins.

Parameters:

rule_type (str, optional) – Filter rules by type (‘tidy’ or ‘rewrite’). None loads all rules.
